Skill: day-one-answer

**Must**
- Leads with a one-sentence answer to the key question, stated as a hypothesis to test
- Lists three to five things that must be true for the answer to hold, each with the analysis or data that would test it
- Marks every assumption and states that no data has been seen yet
- Asks at most three specific clarifying questions, each with a default (for example: what does "flat" mean, in volume or value, and over which period)
- Names the stakeholder to pre-wire first
- Saves the output under `outputs/` and names the next step with an owner

**Must not**
- Invent market data, benchmarks, or competitor names
- Present the hypothesis as a finding
- Hedge with "perhaps" or "maybe consider"
