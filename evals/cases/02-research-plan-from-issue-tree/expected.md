Skill: research-plan

**Must**
- Starts from the issue tree branch and the hypothesis it tests, and writes a five-line research brief (decision, user, format, due date, depth)
- Locks definitions before any search: what counts as cold-chain, which geography is Region A, which time frame, which unit (revenue, tonnage, or pallets)
- Writes MECE research questions for size, growth, and competitors, ranked by impact on the answer
- Gives each question a source and method, and marks the questions the open web cannot answer, with the expert or client data needed
- Runs the confidentiality and compliance check and states that the client is private, so listed-company rules apply only to listed companies researched
- Plans a midpoint check-in and says who gets it
- Asks for approval of the plan before any search runs

**Must not**
- Start searching before the plan is approved
- Quote a market size or growth figure that is not from a logged source
- Treat vendor blogs or press releases as proof of a number
