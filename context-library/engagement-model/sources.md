# Sources and Reliability

Research compiled October 2026. None of these are official internal playbooks. Firms keep those private. Treat as public-domain practitioner knowledge.

## Reliability Key
**A** = published by the firm or by a partner-level author for a university course · **B** = established practitioner or coaching publisher · **C** = single-author or vendor blog, anecdotal · **P** = press report quoting firm leaders

## Known Gaps
- No public source gives a firm's exact internal review SLAs, staffing formulas, or fee tables. Defaults in this folder are working assumptions. Overwrite them in `context-library/engagement-info-template.md`.
- Hours-per-week and time-allocation figures are anecdotal.
- AI figures change quickly and press reports conflict.
