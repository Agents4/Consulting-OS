# Kickoff Meeting and First-Two-Weeks Plan

## A. Kickoff Meeting (60-90 min)

**Date/time:** [ ]  ·  **Attendees:** [Sponsor, counterpart, SteerCo, workstream leads, our team]

| Time | Item | Lead | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Why we are here; sponsor's view | Sponsor | Shared purpose |
| 0:10 | Objectives: the single question | Partner | Agreed key question |
| 0:20 | Scope: in and out; constraints | EM | Boundaries |
| 0:30 | Approach, phases, deliverables | EM | Plan understood |
| 0:45 | Roles and decision rights; who signs off | EM | RACI |
| 0:55 | Governance and cadence: SteerCo, check-ins, status | EM | Calendar |
| 1:05 | Data and access: what exists, who provides, by when | EM / Counterpart | Data owners named |
| 1:15 | First 3-5 tasks and owners | EM | Next steps |

Hold opinions on findings you do not have yet.

## B. Before Day 1
- [ ] Read SOW twice (second time looking for vague areas); list questions
- [ ] Research client and sector; set news alerts
- [ ] Ask partner/EM about working styles
- [ ] Ask who the sponsor is, who feels threatened, who wants this to succeed
- [ ] Draft stakeholder map

## C. First-Two-Weeks Plan
| Days | Focus | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-day 1 | Prep | SOW, team, and politics reviewed |
| 1-2 | Kickoff | Scope, roles, governance, first tasks written down |
| 1-3 | Data | Requests sent |
| 3-7 | Workplan | Hypothesis-driven plan with owners exists |
| 7-10 | Early win | One useful page reaches the client |
| 10-14 | Rhythm | Weekly cadence runs without effort |

## D. Logistics
- [ ] Badges, network, and system access
- [ ] Team room; equipment; client slide template
- [ ] Support staff booked (designer, analyst, researcher)
- [ ] Shared folders on approved platform; naming conventions
- [ ] Calendar holds: check-ins, partner sessions, SteerCos (2-3 months ahead)

Core hours · On-site days · Check-in/out times · Tools · Review SLAs · Feedback norms · Leave and travel
